Our Story

Born on the Equator,
Built for Kenya

TalesUber was founded by Finley Nyabuga right here in Maseno, Kisumu County. Frustrated by unreliable and overpriced transport options for students and locals, Finley set out to build a ride platform rooted in local needs — transparent fares in Kenyan Shillings, verified local drivers, and service that truly understands the Maseno-Kisumu corridor.

Today TalesUber serves thousands of riders across Maseno, Kisumu, Ahero, Luanda and beyond, connecting communities with safe, affordable, and dependable rides every day.
